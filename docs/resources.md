# Resource Notes

TradeTalk 10 uses external resources as optional launch points, not as required daily homework.

## Daily Listening

- VOA Learning English: slower news and learner-friendly audio.
- VOA Let's Learn English: beginner-friendly structured course.
- VOA English in a Minute: short idiom and colloquial expression videos.
- BBC Learning English: extra listening through short recurring programmes.

## Business and Foreign Trade Communication

- British Council Business English: business listening, reading, emails, and workplace communication.
- British Council English for Emails: practical email structure and phrases.
- Business English Pod: meetings, telephoning, presentations, negotiations, sales, and workplace vocabulary.

## AI and Open-Source References

- ChatGPT Voice: customer role-play and oral correction.
- YouGlish: real examples of pronunciation in videos.
- Language Reactor: YouTube/Netflix subtitle replay and pause workflow.
- English Trainer: open-source video-to-listening/shadowing/pronunciation trainer.
- Shadowing English: local-first shadowing app with user-owned videos and subtitles.
- Life Level-up Guide: English learning path and AI-assisted learning workflow.

## Selection Principle

The learner should not browse these resources every day. The app already contains the daily learning path. These links are for:

- replacing a weak daily material;
- weekend extra practice;
- future app upgrades;
- checking pronunciation or business context.
