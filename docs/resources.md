# Resource Notes

TradeTalk 10 uses external resources as optional launch points, not as required daily homework.

## Daily Listening

- British Council LearnEnglish: free CEFR-based listening, reading, speaking, vocabulary, and interactive practice.
- British Council Listening: graded daily listening practice for A1-C1 learners.
- VOA Learning English: slower news, learner-friendly audio, podcasts, and workplace series.
- BBC Learning English: weekly learner programmes such as Real Easy English and 6 Minute English.
- Breaking News English: current graded news lessons in multiple levels.
- Engoo Daily News: short current news lessons for discussion and 60-second retelling.

## Video and Audio

- TED Talks: short expert talks with subtitles and interactive transcripts.
- TED-Ed: short animated lessons for science, culture, history, and general knowledge.
- TED-Ed Lessons: topic-based video lessons with checks for understanding.
- NPR Podcasts & Shows: real-speed American English listening for weekend practice.

## English News

- AP News: current international news with clear headlines.
- TIME: current affairs and opinion-style reading.
- NPR News: news articles and audio-led reporting.
- VOA As It Is: learning-friendly news-style articles and audio.

## Magazines and Long Reading

- Smithsonian Magazine: culture, history, science, arts, and travel.
- National Geographic: image-rich nature, travel, culture, and science reading.
- Scientific American: science and technology reading for higher-level input.
- McKinsey Insights: business, market, growth, and supply-chain topics.
- Harvard Business Review: management and business English; some content is paid.

## Business and Foreign Trade Communication

- British Council Business English: business listening, reading, emails, and workplace communication.
- British Council English for Emails: practical email structure and phrases.
- Business English Pod: meetings, telephoning, presentations, negotiations, sales, and workplace vocabulary.
- Business English Pod Supply Chain: logistics, tariffs, duties, routing, capacity, and purchasing vocabulary.
- Business English Pod Negotiations: price, terms, trade-offs, concessions, and closing language.

## AI and Open-Source References

- ChatGPT Voice: customer role-play and oral correction.
- YouGlish: real examples of pronunciation in videos.
- Language Reactor: YouTube/Netflix subtitle replay and pause workflow.
- English Trainer: open-source video-to-listening/shadowing/pronunciation trainer.
- Shadowing English: local-first shadowing app with user-owned videos and subtitles.
- Life Level-up Guide: English learning path and AI-assisted learning workflow.

## Selection Principle

The learner should not browse these resources every day. The app already contains the daily learning path. These links are for:

- replacing a weak daily material;
- weekend extra practice;
- future app upgrades;
- checking pronunciation or business context.
