# Learning Flow

## Name

**TradeTalk 10**

Chinese name: **外贸英语 10 分钟开口训练器**

## Positioning

TradeTalk 10 is not a full English course. It is a small daily execution system for export sales conversations.

The app assumes the learner has limited time and weak listening confidence. It therefore prioritizes repeated high-frequency scenes over broad material collection.

## 20-Day Topic Map

1. First meeting and company introduction
2. Understanding customer requirements
3. Product introduction
4. Samples
5. MOQ
6. Quotation
7. Lead time
8. Packaging
9. Payment
10. Shipping
11. Quality
12. Customization
13. Price objection
14. Delivery objection
15. Online meeting
16. Trade show conversation
17. Factory capability
18. After-sales issue handling
19. Follow-up
20. Full inquiry simulation

## Daily 10-Minute Session

- Minute 0-2: blind listen to the five sentences.
- Minute 2-4: read the English and mark missed words.
- Minute 4-7: shadow each sentence twice.
- Minute 7-9: retell the scene in simple English.
- Minute 9-10: copy the AI prompt and start a short customer role-play.

## Review Rule

Do not over-correct. Each session should produce only:

- one most important listening miss;
- one more natural sentence;
- three sentences to repeat tomorrow.

The system is intentionally light. Consistency matters more than volume.
