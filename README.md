# TradeTalk 10

TradeTalk 10 is a lightweight 20-day English listening and speaking trainer for export sales people.

The goal is simple: open one page, follow today's 10-minute card, and practice the exact situations that happen in foreign trade conversations: product introduction, MOQ, quotation, sample, lead time, packaging, payment, shipping, quality, customization, objections, trade shows, and follow-up.

## Why This Exists

Most English-learning plans fail because they ask the learner to choose materials every day. TradeTalk 10 removes that friction:

- one local web app;
- one daily card;
- five useful sentences;
- one 10-minute timer;
- one AI role-play prompt;
- progress and notes saved in the browser.

## Core Loop

The daily loop is the **4R loop**:

1. **Receive**: listen first without looking at Chinese.
2. **Read**: check the English sentence and identify missed words.
3. **Repeat**: shadow the sentence with browser text-to-speech.
4. **Role-play**: copy the prompt into ChatGPT Voice and simulate a customer conversation.

## Learning Metrics

Track only the few metrics that matter for real communication:

- completion rate across 20 days;
- five shadowed sentences per day;
- whether the learner can retell the daily scene for 20-60 seconds;
- the customer question that caused the biggest hesitation.

## How To Use

Open `index.html` in a browser.

Recommended browsers: Chrome or Edge, because browser speech recognition support is better there.

## Sources and Resource Direction

The app links to selected learning resources instead of embedding external copyrighted lessons. It points learners to:

- VOA Learning English for slower input;
- British Council LearnEnglish for business and email practice;
- BBC Learning English for extra listening;
- Business English Pod for meetings, sales, telephoning, and presentations;
- YouGlish and Language Reactor for pronunciation and subtitle workflows;
- open-source projects such as English Trainer and Shadowing English as future upgrade references.
